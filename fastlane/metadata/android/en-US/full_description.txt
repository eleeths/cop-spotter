Police equipment talks. Bodycams, TASERs and camera systems constantly broadcast Bluetooth and Wi-Fi signals so their docks and cars can find them. Cop Spotter listens for those signatures — and for hidden trackers and card skimmers too.

Your phone's own radios run a database of 103 verified hardware fingerprints:

• POLICE GEAR (84) — Axon body cameras and vehicle systems, TASER devices, Flock camera accessories, police radio gear, in-car video systems, police drones and Remote ID broadcasts, ambulance monitors, patrol-car routers and fleet telematics. A contact strobes red/blue with a beep and vibration.
• HIDDEN TRACKERS (10) — AirTags, SmartTags, Tiles and similar tags. Alerted in purple, close-range only, and the pulse quickens as you get closer so you can walk one down.
• CARD SKIMMERS (9) — the cheap Bluetooth serial modules skimmers hide inside pumps and card readers use to phone home. Alerted in amber, close-range only — before your card goes in.

RECEIVE-ONLY, BY DESIGN. Cop Spotter listens to public broadcast advertisements that devices intentionally transmit for discovery. It does not intercept, decrypt, record, or interfere with any communication, and it transmits nothing.

THE HONEST PART. Bluetooth carries a room or two, through a wall if you're lucky — Cop Spotter knows gear is close, it can't point down the block. Gear advertises in bursts to save battery, so a quiet device can be missed. This is an awareness tool, not a safety device. Rules around detection equipment vary by place; the responsibility for where and how you use it is yours.

Free and open source (MIT). No account, no subscription, no ads, no tracking. If it earns its keep, you can support the project from inside the app or at eleeth.com — the same signature engine also runs on a pocket hardware unit sold there.
